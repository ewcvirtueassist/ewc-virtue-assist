Our Story

Built on listening first, then matching with care

George started with a small call center in the Philippines in 2014 and formed EWC in 2018. Before that he was a nurse in the US — working in psychiatric care, nursing homes, and hospice.

That background shapes how we work: listen first, understand the person and the work, then match with care. Caroline, who is Filipina, helps bridge culture and language so small misunderstandings do not become big ones.

If the fit is not right, tell us at any time. We find you a replacement within 7 days. Our clients stay because we work on trust — and some have been with us for over five years.

What you can expect

  • Your assistant works through EWC. We handle payroll; you direct the daily work.
  • Communication stays simple: email + Google Meet or Zoom. Use the tools you already have.
  • We do not train the assistants. We source and match the best-fit person, then verify setup and references.
  • Specialist skills (e.g. AutoCAD) may be priced slightly higher — always agreed before you start.
